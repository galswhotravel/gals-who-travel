the trips

4 questions

Every Gals Who Travel trip includes:

✓ Accommodation — carefully selected to match the vibe and location
✓ All itinerary activities — every experience listed on your trip page
✓ Airport transfers — we'll meet you on arrival
✓ A dedicated trip leader — with you throughout the entire trip

Select meals may be included depending on the destination — check your individual trip page for the full breakdown. Flights and travel insurance are not included and must be arranged separately.

Flights are not included — and this is intentional. It means you have the freedom to book from your nearest airport, find the best deal, and use your preferred airline or points.

Once you're booked, we'll send you all the arrival information you need — including which airport to fly into, what time to arrive, and how you'll be met on landing.

Most trips are based on shared twin rooms — this keeps the cost down and honestly helps you bond with your roommate (who will very likely become one of your favourite people on the trip).

Single supplement upgrades are available on most trips for those who prefer their own space. Just drop us a message when you book and we'll let you know availability and pricing for your trip.

Our groups are intentionally small — typically between 10 and 18 women. This is one of the things our travellers consistently mention in their reviews.

Small groups mean you actually get to know everyone properly, logistics run smoothly, and the whole experience feels personal rather than like a package tour. No massive crowds, no getting lost, no waiting around.

booking & payments

3 questions

Head to our trips page, find the departure date that works for you, and secure your spot with a £200 deposit. Bookings are handled securely through our partner TruTravels.

If you're not sure which trip is right for you, contact our team and we'll help you figure out the best fit.

Yes. You secure your place with a £200 deposit, and the remaining balance can be paid in monthly instalments — making it much easier to budget for your trip without paying everything upfront.

The full balance is due 10–12 weeks before departure. To set up a payment plan, email our team after booking and we'll sort it for you.

Cancellations made more than 12 weeks before departure will receive a full refund minus the £200 deposit. Cancellations within 12 weeks may be subject to partial or full loss of payment depending on costs already incurred.

This is exactly why we strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance — a good policy will cover cancellation costs if you need to pull out due to illness, injury, or unforeseen circumstances.

practical info

2 questions

Visa requirements depend on your destination and your nationality — so there's no one-size-fits-all answer here. We include destination-specific visa guidance in your pre-trip information pack.

Ultimately, it's your responsibility to ensure you have the correct travel documentation. We recommend checking your government's official travel advice website and doing this well in advance of your departure date — some visas can take weeks to process.

If you're unsure, ask our team and we'll point you in the right direction.

Once you're booked, we'll send you a destination-specific packing guide tailored to your trip. In general, pack light, breathable clothing, a solid pair of walking shoes, swimwear, and any destination-specific items (like a light layer for temple visits in Asia).

Our golden rule: if in doubt, pack less. You'll almost always be able to pick up anything you need once you're there — and a lighter bag makes everything easier.
