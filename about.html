about us ✨

a solo trip & a dream of creating a space for gals to find friends to travel with

our gals who travel story starts in 2022 with our founder and solo traveller brontë king, a trip to bali and a dream of connecting gals around the world. after travelling solo, staying in hostels and meeting other girls travelling, the same conversations always came up: "i wish i could meet other girls to travel with." "i'd love to visit there but i wouldn't feel safe going solo." brontë wanted to create a safe space for women to travel together, make lasting friendships and support local communities through travel whilst doing it. to give gals the confidence and experience of travelling to new destinations without the worry and stress of finding friendships or routes by themselves.

our founder

for the gals, by the gals

what started as 1 group trip announcement, 8,000 followers on our @galswhotravel instagram and 1 gal hoping some gals would book on to join her girls trip to bali has grown into thousands of women trusting us and travelling with us every year.

partnering with operator trutravels in 2022, we had a shared ethos and dream of changing the world together. since then, gals who travel has grown into a global community of gals meeting irl, connecting and seeing the world safely and affordably with local guides in each destination. now operating 15 different trips across asia, latin america, europe, oceania and africa!!!

and honestly

for all our gals, globally that have a dream.

we wanted to create adventures that give you the opportunity and confidence to come alone. nearly 90% of gals come solo and 100% leave with a new group of friends!!!

travel is more than just the girls who book on our trips, it's the women around the world too. where we can, we hire local female guides, visit women's cooperatives and support women-owned businesses!!!

what we're passionate about

community first

our gals who travel community are the forefront of everything we do. every destination, every style of travel comes from you. we're here to connect women around the world and help you form lifelong friendships whilst seeing the world!

founded by the gals, for the gals

founded by brontë king, gals who travel is a community built around female friendships, world adventures and providing a safe and empowering space for women to connect and travel the world together. small group adventures, max group size 20.

safe, social travel for solo gals!!

for the gals who can't get the trip out of the group chat, that have no one to travel with, or just the girls who want to experience more safely and socially! around 90% of our travellers come solo and almost all of them leave with best friends for life!!!

bucket list activities

sunrise yoga, local family cooking classes, castaway island experiences — every single one of our tours features incredible moments you'll remember forever.

safe & fully covered

atol & abta protected. £200 deposit to secure your place. flexible payment plans included.

local guides

we think the best way to travel is through the eyes of the locals that live there. by using local guides in nearly all our destinations, they'll show you all the hotspots and hidden gems. immerse yourself in local culture and support local communities!
